# Human Judgment in the Agentic Terminal

### An Independent Technical and Human-Centered Analysis of AI-Assisted Linux Administration

**Author:** Britany Taylor  
**Publication:** Independent Research, 2026  
**Status:** Published

---

## Overview

As AI systems move beyond generating suggestions and begin executing tasks, the relationship between human judgment and machine action becomes increasingly important.

*Human Judgment in the Agentic Terminal* examines that relationship through the lens of AI-assisted Linux administration.

Using *Agentic AI for Linux Administration* by Imran Afzal as its subject of analysis, this paper explores the role of human oversight, responsibility, decision-making, and judgment in environments where AI systems can interpret instructions and take action.

The central question is not simply what an AI agent can do, but how we preserve meaningful human judgment when we allow it to do more.

## Research Focus

This independent research examines seven chapters of analysis across the broader intersection of:

- Human judgment and decision-making
- AI agent autonomy and oversight
- Responsibility and accountability
- AI-assisted Linux administration
- Human-centered approaches to agentic systems

## Why This Research Matters

The evolution of AI-assisted systems raises questions that extend beyond technical capability.

When an agent can act, execute, and make operational decisions, human involvement cannot become an afterthought. Understanding where oversight belongs, who remains accountable, and how people retain meaningful control is part of building systems that deserve our trust.

This paper approaches those questions from both a technical and human-centered perspective.
